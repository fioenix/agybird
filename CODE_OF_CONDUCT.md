# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participate with respect, technical honesty, and consideration for people with different backgrounds and experience. Give actionable feedback, distinguish evidence from inference, and focus criticism on the work rather than the person.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and publication of another person's credentials or private data are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when conduct threatens the community or project. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile. Do not place sensitive details in a public issue.

Maintainers will handle reports as confidentially as practical, evaluate relevant context, and choose a proportionate response.
